FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Jetaway a couples trip planner or just a packing app?

Both! It’s a couples getaway planner that lets both of you work together to plan your trip, shares days and plans first, with mine/yours/ours packing and a budget that you both can see.

How is this different from Notes, shared Sheets, or a group chat?

Those weren’t built for purposefully planning a trip. Jetaway keeps the itinerary, packing, and budget in one shared space so you can stay on track and not book last minute.

Do we both need accounts?

Yes both of you need accounts to use Jetaway. You can invite your partner to join you in planning.

Can we keep some packing items private?

Yes. Dual packing supports mine, yours, ours, either, and private items. You can add items to your own packing list that you don't want to share with your partner.

Does Jetaway connect to our bank?

No. Jetaway's budgets are manual envelopes with gentle overage cues. You can add your own budget items to your own envelope.
